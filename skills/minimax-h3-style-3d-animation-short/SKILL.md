Stage one story beat as a stylized 3D performance. Give the character a recognizable silhouette and an expressive pose. Establish where the character, prop and camera begin, then describe anticipation, contact, and the resulting reaction. Use material and lighting details that keep the character readable in motion. At each cut carry forward the facing direction, position of the prop and last pose. Time one audible cue to the action it belongs to. For a shot from a longer film, use the supplied entry and exit poses.
