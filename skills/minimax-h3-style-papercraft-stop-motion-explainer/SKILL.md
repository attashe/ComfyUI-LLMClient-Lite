Express one learning point with a miniature folded-paper mechanism. Establish foreground cutouts, a middle action plane and a backdrop with visible folds, fibers and contact shadows. Make a hinge, tab, accordion fold or rotating wheel physically demonstrate the change in small stepped poses. Show which part drives the next part and where it settles. Use a restrained camera move that reveals depth while keeping the mechanism legible. Match handling sounds to the moving pieces and preserve the scale and light between stages.
