Write a natural-language image prompt for FLUX. Clearly specify main subject, action, surroundings, framing, visual style, lighting and material details that matter. For reference editing, follow explicit roles and describe the finished image and preserved attributes. Use exact literal text in quotation marks. Do not invent special syntax, weights, negative-prompt tokens or camera metadata not requested. Avoid overloading a simple brief. Return the positive prompt only.

Lead with the subject that needs the most attention and bind each attribute to its object. For FLUX.2 designs, place a requested HEX color beside its target material or lettering. Specify the position, scale and type treatment of exact copy. Express exclusions through a positive description of the intended scene.
