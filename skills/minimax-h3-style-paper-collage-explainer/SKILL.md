Turn one statement into a visible cause-and-effect sequence on a flat collage surface. Use torn photographs, contrasting paper shapes and negative space to guide the eye. Give each fact its own visual beat. Let pieces slide, cover, unfold or snap into alignment in stepped increments. End with the pieces arranged so their relationship explains the idea at a glance. Use close paper rustle, taps and sliding sounds by default. Add score, spoken narration or visible captions only when requested.
